2. **Write one versioned run contract:** identify source commit, data freeze, seven-mode formula versions, candidate and trial family, pair/portfolio metric denominators, cost inputs, graph stage IDs, and every gate receipt. Keep `DRAFT_BLOCKED` until its prerequisites are bound.
3. **Reconcile Gate 1, then Gates 2 and 3:** verify the 160-capture archive as data evidence; produce the current cost-coverage and dashboard parity gap matrices. Use the archive for eligibility and limited screening, not a durable independent performance claim.
4. **Decide the numerical policy before Gate 4 acceptance:** the proposed conservative rule is exploratory screening under the manifest and final research acceptance only when both the manifest and stricter `research.yaml` targets pass on the same run. The owner may adopt another explicit versioned mapping. Until then, the final acceptance gate remains blocked.
5. **Run frozen, read-only research only after prerequisites pass.** Publish negative results and incomplete evidence as clearly as passes. Do not advance to shadow, ML/RL, Testnet, or live on a historical validation `PASS`.
